# Master Color Palettes & Design Themes Reference

This reference provides production design themes, color token scales, and aesthetic directions across all business archetypes. Every palette is pre-calculated to exceed **WCAG AA contrast standards (≥ 4.5:1)** against both dark and light surfaces.

---

## 1. Food & Beverage / Restaurants & Hospitality

### Theme 1A: Obsidian Charcoal & Warm Saffron Gold (Recommended)
* **Vibe:** Modern high-end nightlife, highway stop, neon sign glow, warm intimate dining ambiance.
* **Palette Tokens:**
  * Background Main: `#0B0F17` (Deep Obsidian)
  * Surface Container: `#131926` (Midnight Slate)
  * Primary Accent: `#F59E0B` (Warm Saffron Gold)
  * Hover / Focus: `#D97706` (Amber Ochre)
  * Dietary / Success: `#10B981` (Emerald Green)
  * Non-Veg / Alert: `#EF4444` (Spicy Crimson)
  * Typography Main: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Crisp White)
  * Typography Secondary: `#94A3B8` (Muted Blue-Gray)
* **Typography Pairing:** `Outfit` (Headings) + `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Body).

### Theme 1B: Tuscan Terracotta & Warm Olive Cream
* **Vibe:** Rustic Italian trattoria, clay tandoor oven, heritage family recipes, warm welcoming daylight.
* **Palette Tokens:**
  * Background Main: `#FAF7F2` (Warm Milk Cream)
  * Surface Container: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Card Surface)
  * Primary Accent: `#C25E3E` (Burnt Terracotta)
  * Secondary Accent: `#556B2F` (Olive Herb)
  * Typography Main: `#1F1916` (Deep Espresso)
  * Typography Secondary: `#6B5E55` (Warm Earth Gray)
* **Typography Pairing:** `Playfair Display` (Headings) + `Inter` (Body).

## 2. Luxury & Artisanal Brands (Macro-Archetype 1: C2)

### Theme 2A: Onyx Noir & Brushed Platinum
* **Vibe:** Ultra-exclusive atelier, serialized 1-of-1 timepieces, editorial haute horlogerie.
* **Palette Tokens:**
  * Background Main: `#050505` (True Jet Black)
  * Surface Container: `#111111` (Matte Carbon)
  * Primary Accent: `#D4AF37` (Muted Champagne Gold)
  * Platinum Trim: `#E5E7EB` (Brushed Silver)

  * Typography Secondary: `#71717A` (Neutral Gray)
* **Typography Pairing:** `Cinzel` or `Cormorant Garamond` + `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
