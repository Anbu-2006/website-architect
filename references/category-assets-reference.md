# Master Category Asset & Media Standards Reference

This guide outlines the essential visual assets, photography guidelines, iconography, and semantic roles required to create a world-class, high-converting, and aesthetically stunning website across each of the 8 Macro-Archetypes.

---

## Universal Baseline Asset Requirements

Regardless of category, every professional website requires:
1. **Primary Brand Logo:** High-resolution transparent SVG or PNG (horizontal layout, min height 80px).
2. **Submark / Favicon:** Square SVG or PNG (32×32 / 64×64 / 192×192) for browser tabs and mobile bookmarking.
3. **Primary Hero Visual:** Cinematic, category-specific visual (1920×1080 or 2560×1440, WebP format, <250 KB).
4. **Cohesive Vector Icon Set:** SVG stroke-based icons (Lucide, Heroicons, or Material) sharing unified 2px stroke weight.
5. **Brand Color Swatches:** Explicit Primary, Secondary, Background, Surface, and Accent HEX/HSL codes.

## Category-Specific Asset Checklists & Semantic Roles

### 1. Food & Beverage / Restaurants & Hospitality (Macro-Archetype 2 Variant / Local Commerce)
*To look "cool" & award-winning: Full-bleed natural lighting food photography, warm editorial tones, steam/texture closeups, clean typography, live open/closed indicators, and zero downloadable PDF menus.*
* **Essential Visual Assets:**
  * `BrandLogoPrimary`: Transparent logo with clean contrast against both dark and light backdrops.
  * `HeroFoodSignature`: 1–2 stunning, high-res hero images of the primary signature dish (e.g., steaming Dum Biryani, Arab Mandi platter, or woodfired pizza).
  * `DishHighlights [1..6]`: High-appeal dish photos for top menu categories (Tandoor, Curries, Mojitos/Beverages, Desserts).
  * `AmbianceOrInterior`: 1–2 photos of the dining hall, family seating, or scenic highway exterior.
  * `TakeawayOrPackaging`: Photo of branded delivery box, drive-through pickup counter, or curbside staff.
  * `TrustBadges`: Google Maps 4+ star rating badge, hygiene certification mark, Halal / FSSAI / vegetarian emblems.
* **Semantic Tagging Roles:**
  `[Logo]`, `[HeroDish]`, `[FeaturedDish]`, `[AmbianceInterior]`, `[ExteriorHighway]`, `[BeverageDessert]`, `[TrustBadge]`

---

### 2. Direct Commerce & Consumer Retail (Macro-Archetype 1: C1, C2, C3)
*To look "cool" & award-winning: Asymmetric bento grids, serialized macro-detail shots, 360/interactive product cards, clean studio cutouts with soft drop-shadows, and micro-animated cart interactions.*
* **Essential Visual Assets:**
  * `BrandLogoPrimary`: Minimalist vector logo.
  * `HeroProductEditorial`: Lifestyle hero banner showing the product in aspirational, real-world context.
  * `ProductStudioCutouts [1..8]`: Transparent background PNGs (or uniform neutral gray/white) shot from front, 45-degree angle, and packaging.
  * `TextureOrMaterialMacro`: Extreme macro closeup showing material grain, fabric weave, or metal finish.
  * `SocialProofUGC`: 3–4 customer lifestyle photos or unboxing stills.
* **Semantic Tagging Roles:**
  `[Logo]`, `[HeroBanner]`, `[ProductCutout]`, `[MaterialTexture]`, `[LifestyleContext]`, `[PackagingUnbox]`

---

### 3. High-Trust Advisory & Professional Services (Macro-Archetype 3: A1, A2, A3)
*To look "cool" & award-winning: Warm monochrome portraits, architectural office photography, serif/sans-serif editorial typography pairing, strict white space, and zero cheesy generic stock handshakes.*
* **Essential Visual Assets:**
  * `BrandLogoPrimary`: Elegant serif or geometric sans vector mark.
  * `PartnerOrExecutivePortraits`: Authentic, high-end environmental portraits of leadership (not generic passport cutouts).
  * `OfficeArchitecture`: Minimalist interior architecture shots of conference rooms, library, or headquarters.
  * `CredentialBadges`: Bar associations, Chambers & Partners, Forbes Councils, FINRA / SEC / HIPAA badges.
  * `CaseStudyGraphics`: Clean infographic charts illustrating deal volume, capital deployed, or patient outcomes.
* **Semantic Tagging Roles:**
  `[Logo]`, `[PartnerPortrait]`, `[OfficeArchitecture]`, `[CredentialBadge]`, `[CaseStudyGraphic]`

---

### 4. Industrial, Supply Chain & B2B Distribution (Macro-Archetype 4: B1, B2, B3)
*To look "cool" & award-winning: Dark mode technical interfaces, high-density parametric tables, CAD drawing line-art blueprints, factory floor automation imagery, and ISO certification watermarks.*
* **Essential Visual Assets:**
  * `BrandLogoPrimary`: Industrial-grade bold vector mark.
  * `FactoryFloorOrRobotics`: High-contrast photography of CNC machining centers, chemical reactors, or robotic packing.
  * `ComponentCutouts`: Clean product photos of industrial fasteners, raw ingots, valves, or electronic modules.
  * `CADBlueprintSchematic`: Vector SVG or high-res schematic drawing with dimensional callouts.
  * `Certifications`: ISO 9001, AS9100, RoHS, REACH, CE compliance badges.
* **Semantic Tagging Roles:**
  `[Logo]`, `[FactoryMachinery]`, `[ComponentSKU]`, `[CADSchematic]`, `[CertificationBadge]`

---

### 5. Product-Led SaaS & Developer Infrastructure (Macro-Archetype 5: P1, P2, P3)
*To look "cool" & award-winning: Sleek dark mode canvas, glowing glassmorphic UI card mockups, interactive terminal code snippets, animated product feature demos, and customer logo marquee wall.*
* **Essential Visual Assets:**
  * `BrandLogoPrimary`: Modern tech vector mark with monochrome and colored variants.
  * `ProductUIMockups`: High-fidelity desktop & mobile UI screenshots placed inside border-gradient glass frames.
  * `FeatureSpotlightIcons`: 3D-rendered or dual-tone vector icons representing core platform engines.
  * `ClientLogoWall`: Monochrome SVGs of recognizable enterprise customers.
  * `InteractiveTerminalGraphic`: Styled code block or syntax-highlighted terminal window preview.
* **Semantic Tagging Roles:**
  `[Logo]`, `[ProductUIMockup]`, `[FeatureIcon]`, `[CustomerLogo]`, `[TerminalGraphic]`

---

### 6. Field Services & Local Contracting (Macro-Archetype 8: F1, F2, F3)
*To look "cool" & award-winning: High-visibility emergency dispatch ribbons, real technician team portraits with branded service trucks, before/after job comparison sliders, and verified license badges.*
* **Essential Visual Assets:**
  * `BrandLogoPrimary`: High-contrast logo visible on truck wraps and uniforms.
  * `BrandedFleetOrTrucks`: Photo of service vehicles parked outside or on-site.
  * `TechnicianActionPortraits`: Real licensed technicians operating diagnostic gear, welding, or installing equipment.
  * `BeforeAfterProjectPair`: Side-by-side photo comparison of a completed HVAC/plumbing/roofing installation.
  * `TrustBadges`: State contractor license number, BBB A+ rating, 24/7 emergency dispatch emblem.
* **Semantic Tagging Roles:**
  `[Logo]`, `[ServiceFleet]`, `[TechnicianAction]`, `[BeforeAfterProject]`, `[LicenseBadge]`

## Asset Ingestion & Tagging Semantic Schema

When user-uploaded assets are analyzed, each file must be cataloged in the PRD with:
1. `SourcePath`: Location in project directory (e.g., `assets/biryani.jpg`).
2. `SemanticRole`: System identifier (e.g., `HeroFoodSignature`).
3. `TargetComponent`: Specific website component (e.g., `HomepageHero`, `MenuCard_1`, `HeaderNavbar`).
4. `AltText`: SEO and accessibility descriptive text.
5. `Dimensions & Format`: Verified resolution and file type.
