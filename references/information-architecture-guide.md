# Information Architecture & Spatial Wireframe Specification

This reference guide establishes the authoritative spatial wireframe, section hierarchy, and component geometry for websites generated by `/website-architect`. 

Every layout follows a proven descending continuum from **Cognitive Orientation** to **Active Consideration**, **Risk Mitigation**, and **Conversion Commitment**.

---

## 1. Above-the-Fold Geometry & Hero Construction

The viewport above the fold must resolve three fundamental questions within 3.5 seconds:
1. **What does this business deliver?** (Clear commercial capability)
2. **Why is this provider credible?** (Authority & proof)
3. **What action must the visitor take next?** (Primary North-Star CTA)

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [Logo]  🟢 Open Now · Closes 11 PM    [Nav Links]       📞 Hotline    [CTA]   🛒 Bag (0)│ (Utility Header)
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [Tag: Highway Stop · Family Dining]                  │                                 │
│                                                      │                                 │
│ Authentic Dum Biryani & Mandi                        │      [Hero Visual Card]         │
│ on Kanakapura Main Road.                             │      Authentic Photo / Media    │
│                                                      │                                 │

│ generous portions and honest highway family pricing. │   │ 📍 Live Location Badge   │  │

```

### 1.1 The Universal Utility Header
- **Fixed Height:** Desktop: `72px–80px`; Mobile: `56px–64px`.
- **Z-Index & Backdrop:** `z-index: 50`, `position: sticky`, `top: 0`, with glassmorphic backdrop-filter blur (`16px`).
- **Brand Identifier (Top Left):** Vector SVG brand logo paired with title and geographic subtitle.
- **Operational Status Badge (Center Left):** Real-time JavaScript badge comparing client time against business hours:
  - Open: Glowing emerald pulse dot + `"Open Now · Closes 11:00 PM"`.
  - Closed: Amber dot + `"Closed · Opens Tomorrow at 12:00 PM"`.
- **Global Navigation Nodes (Center):** 4 to 6 concise anchor links (`#menu`, `#amenities`, `#reviews`, `#location`).
- **Direct Hotline (Right Utility):** Visible telephone link formatted with `tel:` URI for instant 1-tap mobile calling.
- **Persistent Cart / Order Trigger:** Floating or navbar cart button with real-time numeric counter badge (`cart-badge-count`) opening an off-canvas drawer.

### 1.2 Hero Section Layout Grid (55/45 Desktop Asymmetry)
- **Primary Value Headline (H1):** 8 to 12 words declaring core offering and geographic market. Avoid vague slogans ("Experience Excellence"). Use concrete value ("Authentic Dum Biryani & Mandi on Kanakapura Road").
- **Supporting Subheadline:** 24 to 36 words detailing execution, turnaround speed, and key differentiators.
- **Dual-Action Conversion Cluster:**
  - *Primary CTA:* High-contrast saturated button driving the primary conversion event ("Order on WhatsApp" or "Schedule Consultation").
  - *Secondary CTA:* Ghost or outlined button offering zero-risk orientation ("View Route in Google Maps" or "Browse Menu").
- **Social Proof Micro-Strip:** Star rating counter (`4.0 ★`), verified review volume (`821+ Reviews`), and price tier tag (`₹200–₹400`).
- **Hero Visual Anchor:** High-resolution authentic photograph or 3D canvas card with floating location badge and ambient shadow.

## 2. Mid-Funnel Narrative Flow

Below the fold, users transition from initial interest to evaluation. Sections must appear in this strict order:

### 2.1 Quick-Action Strip / Utility Banner
- Positioned immediately beneath the hero to capture immediate traveler/commuter utility.
- 3 to 4 horizontal cards highlighting high-friction answers:
  - 🚗 Dedicated Highway Parking (easy access for cars/SUVs)
  - 🥡 Curbside Takeaway Window (order ahead on WhatsApp for fast pickup)
  - ❄️ Air-Conditioned Dining & Open-Air Lawn Seating
  - 🕒 Daily Hours & Exact Highway Mile Marker

### 2.2 Core Capability Pillars / Amenities
- 3 to 4 structured feature cards presenting operational strengths.
- Each card contains: Vector icon (48×48px), bold H3 title, and 2-sentence explanatory paragraph.
- Hover micro-interactions: Smooth `translateY(-4px)` with subtle gold glow box-shadow.

### 2.3 Interactive Showcase Engine
The centerpiece of engagement. Tailored strictly to the archetype:
- **Food & Beverage:** Reactive digital menu with live search bar, dietary filter buttons (All / 🟢 Veg Only / 🔴 Non-Veg), category chips (Biryani, Starters, Curries, Drinks), and instant `+ Add to Order` buttons.
- **Contractors & Services:** Interactive Job Cost Estimator or before/after image comparison slider.
- **B2B SaaS:** Interactive pricing tier switcher (Monthly/Annual) with feature breakdown matrix.
- **Portfolio / Agency:** Filterable project showcase grid with hover video previews.

### 2.4 Social Proof & Validation Engine
- Google Maps Verified Review Cards displaying reviewer name, Local Guide badge, star rating, and authentic diner quote.
- Google Review Highlights Strip: keyword badges extracted directly from customer reviews (`"Biryani 29"`, `"Mandi 12"`, `"Highway stop 15"`).
- Press badges or institutional accreditation marquee.

## 3. Bottom-Funnel & Conversion Anchor

The bottom of the page resolves remaining doubts and seals the conversion transaction:

### 3.1 Terminal Conversion Module
- **E-Commerce & Food:** Slide-out Order Bag Drawer with line-item quantities (`+` / `-`), subtotal calculation, delivery/pickup type selector, and single-click WhatsApp order generation.
- **Services & SaaS:** Multi-field lead capture form or Calendly embedded scheduler with name, email, phone, and project scope.

### 3.2 Friction-Mitigation FAQ Accordion
- 4 to 6 expandable accordion cards addressing the most common objections:
  - Parking availability & bus/SUV clearance.
  - Advance ordering & preparation lead times.
  - Dietary accommodations (separate pure-veg cooking).
  - Operating hours and weekend rush policy.
  - Payment modes accepted (UPI, Cards, Cash).

### 3.3 Real-World Location & Logistics Strip
- Responsive two-column layout:
  - *Left Column:* Full physical address, landmark directions, Plus Code, direct hotline, opening hours table, and Instagram link.
  - *Right Column:* High-speed interactive Google Maps embed (`https://maps.google.com/maps?q=...&output=embed`) with rounded borders and lazy-loading.

### 3.4 Persistent Ambient Hotbar (Mobile Viewport Engine)
- Fixed to bottom of screen on viewports `< 768px`: `position: fixed`, `bottom: 0`, `left: 0`, `right: 0`, `height: 64px`, `z-index: 40`.
- 4 equal-width touch targets (minimum 48×48dp):
  1. 📋 **Menu / Services:** Smooth scrolls to showcase.
  2. 📞 **Call:** Immediate phone dialer trigger.
  3. 🗺️ **Directions / Map:** Direct navigation link to Google Maps.
  4. 🛍️ **Order Bag / Book:** Opens slide-out cart drawer or booking modal.

## 4. Single-Page Application (SPA) vs. Multi-Page (MPA) Decision Rules

| Criterion | Choose Single-Page Application (SPA) | Choose Multi-Page Application (MPA) |
|---|---|---|
| **Archetype Match** | Restaurants, Cafes, Highway Stops, Single-Product DTC, Local Trade Contractors, Portfolios | Enterprise B2B SaaS, Multi-Category E-Commerce, Hospitals, Universities, Law Firms with 20+ Practice Areas |
| **Content Depth** | All essential customer information can be consumed in a 4–6 minute continuous scroll. | Deep technical documentation, extensive blog libraries, or complex multi-step legal/medical disclosures. |
| **Primary Conversion Loop** | Immediate impulse: WhatsApp order, phone call, table booking, or quick quote. | Branching funnels: Enterprise demo request, vendor onboarding, career applications, account portal. |
| **SEO Objective** | Hyper-local ranking targeting 1–3 primary local search terms ("best biryani kanakapura road"). | Broad programmatic SEO targeting hundreds of keyword combinations across service categories. |
| **User State Retention** | Cart and booking state must persist seamlessly without page reloads. | Users frequently bookmark specific deep sub-pages or share specific documentation URLs. |

> **Architectural Recommendation:** By default, local businesses, single-location establishments, and boutique services achieve 35% higher mobile conversion rates using a **High-Performance Single-Page Architecture** with slide-out drawers and ambient hotbars rather than disjointed multi-page reloads.
